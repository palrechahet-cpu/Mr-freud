Mr Freud

How it works

Most players get the same psychology concept — they're Psychologists, and they know it cold.

One or two players get a concept that's close but not identical — they're Interns, faking familiarity.

One unlucky player, Mr Freud, gets nothing at all and has to bluff from context alone.

Each round, everyone gives one out-loud clue about their concept — enough to sound informed, not enough to give it away. Then the room votes out whoever seems least convincing.

Only the voted-out player's role gets revealed. If it's Mr Freud, they get one last shot to guess the real concept and steal the win for the outsiders. Otherwise, the room decides for itself whether to keep playing another round or call the game there — there's no automatic cutoff.
